# Portfolio Roadmap: 3 Projects

## Project 1: Global Travel Retail Sales Lakehouse

Status: included in this repository.

Focus:

- Azure Data Factory metadata-driven ingestion.
- Databricks medallion architecture.
- DLT/Lakeflow pipeline.
- Reposted sales dedupe.
- Net sales, enhanced quantity, combo analytics, discount strategy and opening/closing analysis.

Target roles:

- Lead Data Engineer
- Data Architect
- Solution Architect

## Project 2: Energy + IoT Streaming Lakehouse

Suggested repository:

```text
energy-iot-streaming-lakehouse-databricks
```

Focus:

- Streaming meter readings.
- Structured Streaming / Auto Loader.
- DLT expectations and quarantine.
- Unit-level carbon and cost analytics.
- Anomaly detection feature table.
- Dashboard-ready gold model.

Why it helps:

This extends your energy-data experience and shows near real-time streaming architecture, which differentiates you from batch-only candidates.

## Project 3: Inventory & Replenishment Data Product

Suggested repository:

```text
inventory-replenishment-data-product
```

Focus:

- Inventory snapshots and stock movement.
- Sales velocity and demand features.
- Waste and stockout analytics.
- Data product contract and SLA design.
- Dimensional model and semantic layer.

Why it helps:

This connects sales, inventory, and commercial outcomes. It also shows data-product thinking and architectural maturity.
